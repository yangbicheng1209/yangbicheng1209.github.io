About

I am an Associate Professor of Marketing in the Department of Management and Marketing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Before joining PolyU, I was an Assistant Professor at the UBC Sauder School of Business.

I received my PhD in Marketing from the Olin Business School at Washington University in St. Louis, an MA in Economics from Duke University, and degrees in Mathematics and Finance from Nankai University.
